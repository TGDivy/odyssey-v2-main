# Odyssey Constitution

Odyssey exists to support orientation, agency, relationships, health,
capability, learning, and meaning without reducing a life to optimization.

## Product commitments

1. Preserve agency. Offer support, alternatives, uncertainty, and silence;
   never manufacture urgency or shame.
2. Preserve temporal truth. Facts, interpretations, Charter versions, seasons,
   decisions, and corrections retain their history.
3. Earn trust through provenance. Consequential outputs identify their personal,
   scientific, policy, and model basis.
4. Stay useful offline. Capture and core context remain available without cloud
   services, integrations, or language models.
5. Treat relationships as lived commitments, never engagement metrics or ranks.
6. Separate observation, assertion, inference, hypothesis, and accepted meaning.
7. Prefer a few bounded, evaluated interventions over ambient omnipresence.
8. Make correction durable. A correction changes future semantics and is not
   merely an analytics event.
9. Keep derived graph, vector, summary, and model state rebuildable.
10. Export and restore all Odyssey-owned data without proprietary reconstruction.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- a universal Life Score;
- hidden external side effects;
- canonical state owned by an AI provider;
- destructive resets as migration strategy;
- fabricated events, quotes, certainty, or personal causality;
- continuous precise location collection without an approved policy change;
- public or third-party sharing without explicit owner approval;
- silent expansion of sensitive data collection or model-provider routing.

The master specification remains authoritative when this summary is incomplete.
